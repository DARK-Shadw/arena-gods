THE ARENA - AUDIO CREDITS & LICENSES
=====================================

All sound files shipped in audio/sfx and audio/music were trimmed, length-capped,
peak-normalised, converted to mono (sfx) and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis
("changes were made" for attribution purposes). Original archives are not shipped.

1. "Swords & Blades SFX Pack" - The Soundrack
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   (license deed PDF was included in the download)
   Used for: sword whooshes, sword hits/clangs, parries, heavy impacts.
   Modified: trimmed, normalised, re-encoded.

2. "Sword Combat Sound Effects Pack - FREE VERSION" - Hove Audio
   https://hoveaudio.gumroad.com/
   License: NOT STATED in the archive (the included ~README.txt only contains a
   store link). Distributed as a free/royalty-free pack per the downloader.
   ** Confirm the license on the Hove Audio store page before public release. **
   Used for: stab impacts, whooshes, metal collisions, ring shimmer, human effort
   and damage grunts.

3. Kenney (www.kenney.nl) - "Impact Sounds", "RPG Audio", "Voiceover Pack: Fighter"
   License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0) - credit appreciated, not required.
   Used for: punches, body thuds, footsteps, cloth, wood/plate/stone impacts,
   knife slices (arrow hits), announcer lines.
   ("Voiceover Pack #1" was downloaded but is not used.)
   Voiceover Pack credits (as provided): male voice Jeffrey M. Smith,
   female voice Giselle.

4. "Monster Sound Effects Volume 2" - Ogrebane
   License: CC0 per the source page (no license file was included in the archive).
   Used for: creature roars and grunts (pitched down) for Fenrik, Maw, Hollowjack.

5. "Epic Boss Battle [Seamlessly Looping]" - Juhani Junkala
   License: CC0 (per the source page; no license file included).
   Used for: fight music loop (re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis).

6. Character voices (audio/voice/) - generated offline with OmniVoice
   (k2-fsa/OmniVoice, Apache-2.0 model code) in voice-design + voice-clone mode,
   then processed with ffmpeg. Dialogue written for this project.

7. Crowd bed, fire, roars, drums and stingers - synthesised at runtime (WebAudio).
