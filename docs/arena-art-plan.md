# Arena art redesign: plan

Branch `anim-plan`, written 2026-10-18. Status: **A0 done** (harness, calibrated concept cameras, Blender probes, job runner: `docs/milestones/A0/README.md`; corrections from A0 are marked "A0" below); A1 next. Raw notes, machine facts, licence checks and Blender 5.0 API changes are in `docs/arena-art-research.md`.

**Goal (owner, top priority):** the in-game arenas must look as close as possible to the concept art `docs/concept-art/ref-3.png` ... `ref-10.png`, with Blender pushed as far as it goes. The deterministic sim, the generator (theme x layout x seed), destruction, cover, hazards, throwables, entrances and the camera must keep working.

## 0. Summary

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Themes | 8 themes, one per concept image. Seven keep their internal id and get a new look and name; one new id `rootwood` is added (section 2) |
| Hero theme (quality bar) | **The Moon Gate** (id `colosseum`, ref-3), built end to end before any other theme |
| Near-field art (within about 40 m) | Real 3D: a shared modular kit generated by scripted bpy, high-poly detail baked down to low-poly game meshes, trim sheets, scanned CC0 surfaces, exported as GLB + meshopt + KTX2 |
| Far-field art | Cycles-rendered **layered panoramas** of full 3D Blender scenes (landmark, castles, islands), with a depth pass for parallax and AOV masks that animate waterfalls, lava, windows and clouds in the game |
| Floor | One shared wet-stone floor system: scanned flagstone base, Blender-baked inlaid ritual circle per theme, puddle and wetness masks, **planar reflection** of a reflection layer (exact because the floor is one plane) |
| Lights | Fixed real-light count per tier (arena 4/2/1, FX pool 3/2/1), everything else through emissive + bloom, a load-time floor light map and FX glow cards |
| Loading | Pre-baked GLB/KTX2 assets streamed per theme, prefetched from the select screen, cached by the browser; no CPU texture generation; target 1.2 s theme switch from cache |
| Sim | Untouched, except one data row for `rootwood` in `THEME_RULES` (A17) and a frozen seed-to-theme list so old seeds keep their default arenas |
| F4 (fire and environment FX) | Delivered by the arena sessions A19-A20, after F3 lands, using `docs/fx-plan.md` as the spec |
| Size | 23 milestones (A0-A22), each one agent session (at most about 1 agent-day). About 10-15 h of unattended Blender time on top (A0 measured; was 30-40 h), a few hours if the iGPU renders the panoramas |

Order: harness and probes, pipeline, floor, shared kit, backdrop pipeline, **hero theme**, theme kits, the other seven themes, entrances and camera sweep, F4, perf and loading, final review.

## 1. Concept art breakdown

Coordinates in this section are screen positions in the image (0..1 from the top-left). Camera numbers are estimates by eye; A0 calibrates them (section 3.3). The common art language across all eight images:

- a dark, wet, cracked stone floor (mud in ref-6) that mirrors every light source, usually with an inlaid ritual circle centred on the duel;
- many warm fires (braziers on pedestals, candles, lanterns) against a cool sky: blue, violet or overcast grey;
- crimson banners with a gold or bone-white sigil, torn at the bottom, plus heavy hanging chains;
- hooded or armoured colossal statues flanking the view;
- one huge landmark straight behind the opponent, layered against mid-ground ruins and a misty far field (waterfalls, floating rocks, cloud seas);
- a low over-the-shoulder hero camera: the foreground fighter, back to camera, fills the right 25-35% of the frame; the opponent stands 6-9 m away near the centre. (A0 calibration: the cameras are 0.6-1.5 m high, the opponent 5-8.5 m away; the per-image "Camera" estimates below are superseded by `conceptCams.ts`.)

### 3.2 Concept-match checklist (per theme, measured on the concept-camera render)

Every theme milestone ships `docs/milestones/A<n>/concept/<theme>_side.jpg` (concept left, game right), `_blend.jpg` (50% overlay) and `_metrics.json`, and ticks this list. The owner's eye is the final judge; the numbers only track progress and catch regressions.

| # | Check | Measure | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Landmark placement and silhouette | skyline mask IoU over the top 60% of the frame, after the preset is calibrated. **A0:** concept sky = by-eye skyline profile per image (`tools/arena/concept_targets.json`; colour segmentation fails on painterly images), game sky = visual (frame vs sky-only frame); also `profileErr` (mean per-column skyline difference), the useful number when the concept has under ~8% sky (ref-4, ref-6, ref-10) | >= 0.55 (>= 0.65 for the hero) |
| C2 | Palette | mean CIELAB per cell of a 3x3 grid, Delta E against the concept | median <= 12, worst cell <= 20 |
| C3 | Warm/cool split | share of warm pixels (hue 345-360 and 0-50 deg, saturation > 0.4, L > 40; A0 added 345-360 for crimson); cool share reported | within +-40% of the concept |
| C4 | Wet floor | share of bottom-third pixels brighter than L 55 (specular highlights and streaks) | within +-50% of the concept; at least 3 distinct fire streaks visible |
| C5 | Detail density | mean Sobel magnitude per 3x3 cell (catches flat, empty areas) | each cell 0.7-1.3x the concept |
| C6 | Dressing count in frame | braziers, banners, chains, statues, candles visible (counted by the agent on the render) | each within +-30% of the concept count (section 1) |
| C7 | Floor inlay | the ring pattern visible and the right style (engraved / bronze / gold / none) | yes |
| C8 | Atmosphere | ground fog band visible at the far boundary; the far layer has lower contrast than the mid layer (luminance std of far region < 0.6x mid) | yes |
| C9 | Motion (video check) | waterfalls flow, flames flicker, banners sway, particles drift, lava pulses: a 4 s clip from the preset | yes, no popping |
| C10 | Gameplay readability | fighters readable (the existing `cameraAudit` "readable" score not lower than before on the same seeds) | not worse |

### 3.3 Side-by-side comparison method and camera presets

- **`src/render/arena/conceptCams.ts`**: one preset per concept image. `?concept=ref-3` (debug URL) loads the theme and layout, places two fighters in fixed stances at fixed marks, hides the HUD, sets the preset camera, freezes the director, and renders at the concept's pixel size (1672x941; ref-3 at 1793x877).
- **`tools/shots/concept.cjs`** (copy of `shots.cjs` patterns): headless Chromium, `--q=0|1|2`, writes the render and runs **`tools/arena/concept_compare.py`** (Pillow + numpy; run WITHOUT `-I`, numpy is in the user site-packages): side-by-side, blend, metrics C1-C5 + C8 into JSON, contact sheet. Optional `--clip=4` writes a 4 s frame strip for C9. **Built in A0** (`npm run arena:concept`); see `docs/milestones/A0/README.md`.
- **Calibration (A0, done):** `tools/arena/calibrate.py` from the annotations in `tools/arena/concept_targets.json`. Correction to the method below: the line where the far floor meets the boundary is the **floor edge, not the horizon** (it sits well below eye level), and the concept cameras are **low (0.6-1.5 m)**. The solver fits camera height + pitch jointly (opponent as a ruler: feet..head on screen; our arena boundary on the concept's floor edge), yaw from the landmark x, marks by casting the annotated feet onto the floor; the foreground fighter is backed off until an upright rig's head stays in frame (the concepts draw it hunched). ## 4. How the new art attaches to the existing system

### 4.1 Data flow

```
ArenaSpec {theme, layout, seed}
  ├─ sim/arenaGen.ts  (unchanged)  -> ArenaLayout (obstacles, props, hazards, rx/rz, wallH, waypoints)
  └─ render:
       art/<theme>.ts   ThemeArt  (data: which kit assets play which role, sockets, floor, backdrop, lights)
       assets.ts        ArenaAssets.load(theme, tier)  -> GLB/KTX2 cache (kit + theme pack)
       dressKit.ts      generic builder: floor, boundary ring, gates + anchors, backdrop, ambient sockets
       arenaView.ts     unchanged contract: obstacles / props / hazards / topple / crack / gates / applyFrame
```

`dressing.ts` keeps its interface (`Dressing`: mats, `obstacle()`, `propMat()`, `debrisOf()`, `chandelier()`, ...). A new `dressKit(a, art, assets)` implements it from data, so a theme is a ~150-line `ThemeArt` file instead of 300-800 lines of code. The old `dressClassic/Nature/Forge.ts` stay as the fallback behind `?arena=legacy` until A21 removes them.

### 4.2 GLB conventions (what the Blender scripts must export)

| Node name / extras | Meaning | Consumer |
|---|---|---|
| `KIT_<id>` root, `extras: {role, footprint, height, debris, dustTint, tier}` | one asset | `assets.ts` |
| `LOD0`, `LOD1` | tier LODs (LOD1 used on low; no distance LOD in the 40 m near field) | `dressKit.ts` |
| `COL` (hidden, 20-300 tris) | line-of-sight and anchor-snapping proxy | camera LOS, `snapAnchors`, `validateEntrances` |
| `SHAFT` with `extras: {y0, y1}` | stretchable middle section (9-slice in y) so a column or pillar matches the sim's `o.h` exactly without scaling the capital | `obstacle()` builder |
| `SOCK_flame` `{size, palette, light}`, `SOCK_candle`, `SOCK_glow {color, radius}`, `SOCK_chain {to}`, `SOCK_fog {radius}`, `SOCK_heat` | attachment points for FX and lights | arena -> `fx.flame`, cards, chains, fog cards |
| `ANCHOR_perch_<id> {depth}`, `ANCHOR_rim_<id> {drop}`, `ANCHOR_stand_<n>`, `ANCHOR_lowwall {height}`, `TUNNEL {depth, height}` | entrance stage anchors | `makeGate` -> `GateAnchors` |

### 4.3 Sim-owned objects: exact attachment rules

| Sim object | Sim data the art must honour | Art attachment |
|---|---|---|
| Obstacle (cover, toppleable) | `x, z, r, h, kind, hp`; topples about the base edge (`startFall` pivots at `0.8 r`), lands 0.9 s later; crack 0..1 from HP | `ThemeArt.obstacles[kind]` -> kit asset. Footprint radius of the visible base <= 1.55 r (the camera's `standing()` factor; the asset's `footprint` extra replaces the hard-coded factors in `arenaView.standing()`). Height: base + stretched `SHAFT` + capital = `o.h`. Material: the kit material patched with the existing `withCrack` (world-space crack overlay, `materials.ts`). Topple: unchanged pivot code. On landing: the mesh hides as today, and the asset's `FRAC_*` pieces spawn along the fall line (render only, positions from the recorded `dx, dz`), while the sim's two rubble `chunk` props use the theme's `chunk` asset |
| Rock obstacle (indestructible) | `hp 9999`, not toppleable | rubble / boulder asset by seed index |
| Prop | `PROP_BASE` radius; `TOP` height (crate 0.9, barrel 1.0, urn 0.7, rock 0.75, keg 1.0, brazier 1.2, lantern 1.6, chunk 0.6); fragile set; carry offsets (urn 0.36, crate 0.41, else 0.45) | Each prop asset is authored to exactly that radius and top height (an `A1` budget check measures the GLB bounds against `PROP_BASE`/`TOP`). Origin at the base centre. Breaks: the asset's `FRAC_*` pieces feed the debris pool for that kind instead of the generic chunks. Explosive keg: the kit keg plus `SOCK_glow` for the fuse spark (replaces the torus band). Brazier and lantern props: `SOCK_flame` / `SOCK_glow` |
| Hazard | `kind, x, z, r, period, phase`; frame state codes | `ThemeArt.hazards[kind]` picks the visual builder (spike grate / stake trap; lava pool rim; water pond rim; firepit ring; vent; chandelier variant). Chandelier: the asset's hang height **must be 6.2 m** (hard-coded in `hangingNow()` and `main.ts` camWorld; A7 moves the 6.2 into a shared constant), and `D.chandelier(h)` keeps returning one Object3D |
| Gate | `makeGate(a, i, off, build)` returns the portcullis + anchors | gate asset with `BARRIER`, `ANCHOR_*`, `TUNNEL`; `makeGate` reads them through a small adapter; anchors still pass through `snapAnchors` |
| Boundary | `boundR`, `wallH` (camera clamp) | ring modules placed with `alongEllipse`; the wall height seen by the camera must stay close to `wallH` (balustrade top), or `ThemeArt` sets a visual `wallH` used by `camWorld` |

**Generator compatibility:** every layout (pit, oval, cross, split, gauntlet) must dress. The ring builder works on any ellipse (`alongEllipse` with arc spacing). The split layout's river cells and bridges get kit pieces (`bridge` role). Gate lanes stay clear (the generator already keeps them clear). Placement randomness uses `R(seed)` from `noise.ts`, never `Math.random` (the current `banner()` phase uses `Math.random`: fix in A4).

**Camera and anchor compatibility:** `rayProxies()`, `solids()` (`cameraAudit.ts`) and `snapAnchors()` (`entrance.ts`) skip `InstancedMesh` and `userData.dyn`. All instanced kit pieces (stakes, candles, rubble, chain links) must therefore contribute their `COL` proxy to a hidden `arena.losGroup` (plain merged `Mesh`, never rendered: `layers` set to an unused layer), and `solids()` must include `losGroup`. Large static merged meshes still go through `rayProxies` tiling.

### 4.4 Determinism rule

Nothing in `src/render/arena` reads sim RNG or writes sim state. `sim:test` output must be byte-identical before and after every milestone except A17 (which adds `rootwood` rows). Render-side randomness is seeded (`R(seed)`), so screenshots are reproducible.

## 5. The Blender pipeline (pushed to the peak, memory-safe)

### 5.1 Layout and runner

- Scripts: `tools/arena/blender/`. Shared module `abake.py` imports and extends `tools/fx/blender/fxbake.py` (args, dry run, peak RAM via `GetProcessMemoryInfo`, free-RAM guard), adding: `load_cc0(id)` (from `Assets/arena-src/`), `bake_maps(low, high, maps, px)`, `export_glb(obj, path)`, `render_panorama(...)`, `log_step(name)` (RAM + seconds per step into `ram.log`).
- Runner (**built in A0**, Python because it needs Win32 process-memory and suspend calls): `python -I tools/arena/bake.py <job|group|all> [--force] [--list] [--dry]` (`npm run arena:bake -- ...`), jobs in `tools/arena/jobs.json`. One Blender process **per asset job** (process exit frees all memory), strictly sequential. Resumable: each job writes `tools/arena/_bake/<job>/done.json` with a hash of the script, `abake.py`/`fxbake.py`, the job definition and its inputs, and is skipped when unchanged. Waits (then skips) below 4 GB free RAM at start (GPU jobs: 6 GB); a watchdog samples Blender's RAM every 2 s into `ram.csv`, **suspends** Blender below 1.5 GB free, resumes above 2.5 GB, kills it below 0.7 GB, after 20 min paused, or after 3 min paused when the job itself is the hog. It prints the peak RAM per job and keeps `_bake/summary.json`. `abake.py` exists (A0) with `Job` (timed steps + RAM + `result.json`), `bake_maps`, `export_glb`, `cycles_device`, `pano_camera`, `compositor_outputs`; A1 added `load_cc0`, `fit_bounds`, `uv_unwrap`, `bake_emit_inputs`, `pack_orm`, `empty`, `tri_count`, `bounds` (see `docs/milestones/A1/README.md`).
- Never run Blender together with the dev server and a headless browser. A theme session starts the long Cycles renders in the background **and does only code and typecheck work while they run**; screenshots come after.
- Raw CC0 downloads: `Assets/arena-src/<source>/<id>/` (outside git), fetched by `tools/arena/fetch_cc0.mjs` from the Poly Haven and ambientCG APIs (CC0, so agents may script the download) with a ledger `public/arena/credits.json`.
- Output: `public/arena/kit/*.glb`, `public/arena/<theme>/*`, `public/arena/manifest.json`. `_bake/` and `_work/` are gitignored (add to `.gitignore` in A1).

High-to-low bake details:
- Low mesh first (clean procedural topology), high = low + subdivision + displacement / SDF erosion (so the cage is the low itself, cage extrusion 2-4 cm).
- Cycles bake, `use_selected_to_active`: **normal** (tangent, OpenGL convention to match three), **AO** (128 samples), **curvature** (GN edge-angle / pointiness written to a colour attribute, emission bake), **thickness** optional. Albedo, roughness and metal come from the material layer (scan or procedural), modulated by AO and curvature (edge wear lighter, cavities darker and rougher).
- Texel density: 512 px/m for near props and pillars (1024 sets), 256 px/m for ring modules (trim sheet), 128 px/m for the floor tile at 2048 over 4 m.
- UVs: Smart UV Project + `Pack Islands` (5.0 "Shape Method": concave) with 8 px padding at 1024.

### 5.3 Trim sheets and materials

- **One stone trim sheet per stone family** (2048, rows: plain ashlar band, moulding, carved frieze band, rune band, broken edge, plinth), baked from high-poly strip models. All ring modules map to it, so the whole boundary is 1-3 materials and `mergeStatic` turns it into 1-3 draw calls.
- **Scanned base + Blender specifics:** start from CC0 scans (Poly Haven `monastery_stone_floor`, `castle_brick_07`, `castle_wall_slates`, `stone_tiles_02`, `slate_floor_02`, `rock_tile_floor`, `brown_mud_rocks_01`, `forest_leaves_02`, `medieval_wood`, `rusty_metal_03`, `japanese_cedar_bark`; ambientCG `Moss002`, `Bark012`, `Fabric0xx`, `Metal0xx`), then add in Blender what scans do not have: wetness, puddles, cracks aligned to slab seams, soot, moss in cavities, inlays.
- **Procedural shader -> PBR bakes** for the cases with no scan: hammered bronze, gold leaf, lacquered red wood, banner cloth (woven fabric normal + fibre roughness), wax, bone, ice crust, lava crust. Seamless tiling: drive Blender's 4D noise with `(cos u, sin u, cos v, sin v)` (torus mapping) so every noise is periodic; bake with emission passes (1 sample, about 1-3 min per 2048 set).
- **Packing:** albedo (sRGB), normal (RG, Z rebuilt), ORM (AO, roughness, metal) + optional height in the albedo alpha. three's `MeshStandardMaterial` reads `aoMap` (R), `roughnessMap` (G), `metalnessMap` (B) from the same texture.

### 5.4 Statues and colossi (the hardest "sculpt" problem, solved with our own characters)

1. Load a fighter GLB from `public/models` (aldric for knights, vex/ozrel for hooded figures, erika for the goddess) into Blender.
2. Pose it from our own clip data (set pose-bone rotations from the JSON clips in `public/anim`; the 5.0 legacy Action API is gone, so do not rely on `action.fcurves`): knight gripping a sword point down, hooded figure with staff, seated meditation (built by hand-set bone rotations).
3. Add a cloak and hood: a cloth plane pinned at the shoulders, collision with the posed body, 120 frames settle.
4. Join, **voxel remesh** at 1-2 cm (statue scale) or 5 cm (colossus) so body and cloth become one stone monolith; add SDF-grid erosion and chips; apply a stone material with cavity dirt and moss.
5. Decimate to 3-8k tris for the near field (bake normal/AO from the remeshed high), or keep full detail for backdrop renders only.
6. Licence: Mixamo-derived fighters are fine in our own game; check `Models_HF` provenance before using those as statue bases (open question for the owner).

Fallback: Poly Haven `gothic_statue` (27.7k tris) and CC0-marked Smithsonian scans as bases.

### 5.5 Geometry-nodes kitbashing (used where instance counts are large)

- Castle generator for backdrops: curve-driven walls with battlements, towers (cylinder + spire + windows instanced along height), arched viaducts (array of arch modules along a curve), buttresses; windows get an emission material and an AOV mask.
- Floating islands: inverted cone + noise displacement + strata, scatter rocks, trees, ruins on top; waterfalls as emissive-masked sheets off the rim.
- Rubble and debris scatter at wall bases, moss and ivy scatter on top faces (by normal), candle clusters on altars.
- GN `Mesh to SDF Grid` / `SDF Grid Boolean` / `SDF Grid Fillet` / `Grid to Mesh` (new in 5.0) for weathering high-polys.
- Rule: build GN trees from Python with explicit node names and our own seeds; `Realize Instances` only for bakes; keep instancing for Cycles renders (memory).

### 5.6 Backdrop panoramas (the landmark vistas)

Full 3D Blender scenes per theme: landmark (arch + colosseum ring, volcanic citadel, buddha cliff + islands, skull gate + camp + cliffs, snowy castle + viaduct, floating cathedral + rings, goddess + moon castle, tree-city), built from the kit, GN generators, heavy CC0 scans (cliffs, trees: render only) and the theme's HDRI + sun/moon matching `themes.ts`.

Render (Cycles; **A0 proved oneAPI works on the Arc iGPU**, 3-4x faster than the CPU, output identical, but the high layer peaks at 5.7 GB: use the GPU only with 6-7 GB free, else CPU):
- Camera at the arena centre, 1.6 m high, **panoramic equirectangular with latitude limits** (-12 deg to +60 deg), 360 deg longitude.
- **Three depth layers** (separate view layers with holdouts): near-mid (40-120 m: outer walls, close islands), mid (120-600 m: landmark), far (sky, mountains, clouds). Alpha per layer.
- Passes per layer: colour (EXR half), **Depth**, and **AOVs**: `water` (waterfall/flow mask + flow speed), `emit` (lava, windows, distant fires: flicker groups in R/G/B), `cloud` (cloud-sea mask for scrolling).
- Sizes (high tier): 8192x1365 per layer (22.7 px/deg, matching a 1080p frame at 45 deg vFOV); medium 4096x683; low 2048x341, far layer only.
- **Hero inset:** a 4096x2048 perspective render of the landmark as framed in the concept preset, mapped onto the panorama region it covers (sharper where the camera looks most).
- Volumetric fog and god rays: rendered **into the layers** (EEVEE volumes for speed, or Cycles biased volumes), plus separate 512 px **light-shaft and fog-bank card sequences** (EEVEE, 32 frames, looped) for the game's fog cards.
- EEVEE fallback and preview: 8 perspective slices of 45 deg each, stitched onto a cylinder (**A0: EEVEE does not support panoramic cameras**, it renders a PANO camera as plain perspective). Preview every scene with EEVEE slices (measured: 7.4 s per 1024x1365 slice, about 1.2 min for 8, 1.8 GB) before committing the long Cycles render.
- Multilayer EXR per high layer (Image + Depth + one AOV, half float) is about 224 MB: keep in `_bake`, never commit.

In the game (`backdrop.ts`): each layer is a cylinder segment mesh (256 x 32 vertices) at its layer radius, **vertices displaced by the rendered depth** (clamped, smoothed), so moving the camera inside the arena gives real parallax between layers and within the landmark. Fog is applied from the depth so the in-game height fog blends into the panorama. The AOV masks animate: UV-scrolling noise inside `water` (waterfalls flow), noise-modulated emission in `emit` (lava pulses, windows and distant fires flicker), slow offset in `cloud` (cloud sea drifts). The moon and sun discs and a slow cloud layer stay in `sky.ts` (moon sized as in section 3.1), behind the far layer.

### 5.7 Baked lighting, AO and floor masks

- **Per-asset AO and curvature** baked into each kit texture (layout-independent, so it survives the generator).
- **Contact AO** under modules: a soft blob texture placed as part of each asset (`LOD0` child quad, merged).
- **Floor light map at load (GPU, not Blender):** every brazier, candle cluster and lava pool the dressing placed renders an additive radial falloff (warm, with the asset's colour) into a top-down 1024x1024 texture over the floor bounds, once per arena build (well under 1 ms). The floor shader multiplies it by a global flicker uniform from the FX clock. This is the "baked" light pool of dozens of fires at zero per-frame cost, and it adapts to every layout and seed.
- **Wetness and puddle masks:** Blender bakes per floor set a tileable puddle/wetness mask (height-driven: water collects in low areas of the scan's height map) and, per theme, a large non-tiling macro mask (1024, over the arena) so puddles do not repeat. Roughness = mix(stone roughness, 0.05, wetness); albedo darkened by 30-45% where wet; normal flattened in puddles.
- **Inlaid ritual circles:** per theme, the pattern is generated in Python (rings, rune band, compass star, lotus, glyph band) as curves, extruded as bronze/gold strips (or engraved grooves), bevelled, baked against a flat plane: height, normal, AO, metal mask, and a cavity mask. 2048x2048 over a 16 m disc (7.8 mm/px), blended over the flagstones in the floor shader.
- Full per-layout lightmaps (theme x layout = 40 bakes, needing the TS placement logic re-run in Blender) are **not** planned: the cost is high and the load-time floor light map covers the visible part (pools on the floor). Revisit only if A8 shows the ring modules look flat.

### 5.8 Export, compression, LODs, instancing

- Blender glTF exporter (only maintenance changes in 5.0): `export_format='GLB'`, `export_yup=True`, `export_apply=True`, custom properties as extras, no Draco.
- `tools/arena/build_assets.mjs` (gltf-transform 4.x, already a devDependency): `dedup`, `weld`, `prune`, `meshopt` (geometry), textures to **KTX2** (UASTC + zstd for normals and the hero inset; ETC1S for albedo, ORM, masks, far layers) with a **WebP fallback** set; `.low` variants at half resolution. **A0 findings:** KTX-Software **5.0.0-rc2 is installed** (`ktx.exe` only, no `toktx`); gltf-transform 4.x `uastc`/`etc1s` FAIL with it (they pass `--assign-oetf`, renamed `--assign-tf` in 5.0) while exiting 0, so encode with **`ktx create` directly** (flags verified in the A0 README) and pack the `.ktx2` images with the gltf-transform API, or install KTX-Software 4.4.x. gltf-transform `webp` is broken in this install (sharp/libvips): make WebP with Pillow or Blender's exporter (`export_image_format='WEBP'`).
- Runtime: `GLTFLoader` + `MeshoptDecoder` + `KTX2Loader` (transcoder copied to `public/basis/`), `detectSupport(renderer)`.
- LOD: tier LODs (LOD0 on medium/high, LOD1 on low), not distance LODs, because the near field is under 40 m and the far field is panoramas.
- Static ring modules: merged per material (existing `mergeStatic`). Many small repeated items (stakes, candles, chain links, rubble, balusters): `InstancedMesh` with `COL` proxies into `losGroup`.

## 6. Real-time techniques (Three.js r170, WebGL2, Arc iGPU)

### 6.1 Wet floor reflections: options compared

| Option | Quality vs concept | Cost on Arc (est.) | Off-screen content (moon, landmark above the frame) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baked cube probe + box projection + roughness/puddle masks | static, no fighters, no flames | about 0.1 ms | yes (baked) | **low tier** base |
| Reflection streak cards (FX plan 2.5) | stylised streaks under each fire, no geometry | 0.1-0.3 ms | n/a | low tier + accents everywhere |
| SSR-lite on the F1 shared depth (half res, about 16 taps) | contact reflections; misses everything off-screen | 1.0-2.0 ms | **no** | rejected: misses the moon/landmark, which the low camera keeps above the frame |
| **Planar reflection of a `REFLECT` layer** (backdrop, sky, big silhouettes, flame and glow cards via `FX_ADD`, fighters on high), mirrored camera, quarter res (medium) / half res (high), mip chain, `textureLod` by roughness, normal-map distortion, Schlick Fresnel, wetness-weighted | exact for the flat y = 0 floor; long fire streaks appear naturally from reflected flame cards + bloom | 0.5-0.8 ms medium, 1.0-1.5 ms high (to be measured in A2) | yes | **medium/high** |
| Full-scene `Reflector` | best | doubles the scene pass (+6-7 ms) | yes | rejected |

The planar pass needs one hook in `post.ts` before the scene pass (`post.preScene(cb)`): a small render-wide change, agreed with the FX/post owner (F1 is done; F3 is active in `src/render/fx`, not in `post.ts`, but check before editing). The reflection includes `FX_ADD` (the FX plan's requirement), not `FX_ALPHA` or `FX_DIST`.

### 6.2 Many warm torch lights

- Real `PointLight`s per tier, fixed count so shaders never recompile: arena 4 / 2 / 1 (high / medium / low), FX pool 3 / 2 / 1 (FX plan 2.5). The arena picks the flames nearest the arena centre (and the hero frame) for its real lights.
- Every other flame: emissive bowl coals + FX flipbook flame (F2/F3 `unity_flame02/03`) + glow card + its contribution in the **floor light map** (5.7) + its reflection (planar on medium/high, streak card on low).
- Flicker: one deterministic noise per flame (FX clock, `noise1(t * f + seed)`), shared by the real light, the cards and the floor light map's global term.

### 6.3 Fog layers and light shafts

- Keep `fog.ts` height fog, tuned per theme to the panorama's fog (the backdrop uses its depth to match).
- Fog banks: soft-depth fog cards (FX F1 material, `unity_cloud01` and our EEVEE fog-bank sequences) at `SOCK_fog` positions on the boundary and around waterfalls; 0 / 8 / 20 per tier.
- Light shafts: baked shaft card sequences (Rootwood, Celestial Shrine, Frostspire) as additive soft cards aligned to the sun direction; the existing screen-space god rays (high only, when the sun is on screen) add on top.

### 6.4 Backdrop parallax, sky and moon

Depth-displaced cylinder layers (5.6), drawn after opaques with depth test on and depth write off; one draw per layer (3 / 2 / 1 per tier) plus the hero inset. `sky.ts` keeps the procedural dome for the sky gradient, stars, the moon disc (with an HDR glow for bloom) and a slow cloud layer; the far panorama layer sits in front of it with alpha.

### 6.5 Waterfalls, floating rocks, cloth, chains, particles, grade

| Element | Technique |
|---|---|
| Distant waterfalls | AOV `water` mask in the panorama + scrolling noise and foam brightening in the backdrop shader |
| Near waterfalls (Rootwood, Celestial Shrine edges) | a mesh sheet with a flow-map shader (two-phase UV scroll), mist cards at the base |
| Floating rocks (Sanctum, Moonfall) | 10-20 low-poly baked rock meshes (500-2k tris) at 40-150 m, instanced, bobbing and slowly rotating in the vertex shader (seeded phases) |
| Banners | vertex shader sway: displacement scales with distance from the pinned top (`uv.y`), two noise octaves, wind direction per theme; replaces the CPU loop in `common.ts banner()` |
| Chains | catenary between two sockets, instanced links, a slow swing (time-varying sag and lateral offset) in the vertex shader; `chain swing` events optional later |
| Ambient particles | existing `ambient.ts` kinds (embers, motes, petals -> maple leaves, snow, fireflies, dust) plus new `leaves` and `ash`; counts per tier as today |
| Per-theme post grade | `themes.ts` grade (lift/gain/contrast/saturation/tint/vignette) and `fxLook.ts` `THEME_LOOK` (bloom, split toning, black toe), tuned against the concept with the C2/C3 metrics |

### 6.7 Loading strategy (replacing the 3-6 s CPU build)

1. **Baked, not generated:** all textures arrive pre-baked (KTX2/WebP). `textures.ts` CPU generation remains only for the legacy fallback and tiny utility textures.
2. **Per-theme packs:** `manifest.json` lists the shared kit and each theme's files per tier. Only the chosen theme loads.
3. **Prefetch from the select screen:** when a theme card is selected (or "Random" resolves), `ArenaAssets.prefetch(theme, tier)` starts downloading; KTX2 transcoding runs in the loader's web workers.
4. **Cache:** Vite-hashed file names with immutable HTTP caching; optional small service worker (Cache Storage) for offline repeat visits (A21, owner decision). Keep the previous theme in memory while a rematch uses the same theme (`ensureArena` already reuses the same spec).
5. **Shader warm-up:** `renderer.compileAsync(scene, camera)` during the arena reveal splash.
6. **Async build:** `ensureArena` becomes async (`await ArenaAssets.load(...)` then the synchronous `new Arena(...)`); `start()` in `main.ts` is already async. A loading veil covers the wait on a cold cache.
7. **Dispose:** textures and geometries of the old theme disposed on switch (fixes the "textures leak when switching themes" known issue).

Targets: theme switch from cache <= 1.2 s (high); first visit about 22 MB (high) = about 3.5 s on 50 Mbit/s, overlapped with fighter selection; low tier first visit about 8 MB.

**Why the Moon Gate is the hero theme:**
1. It uses the most shared-kit elements of any image: wet floor with ring inlay, braziers, banners hung from chains, chains, hooded statues, stairs, arches, rubble, moon backlight, ground fog. Building it proves the kit for everyone else.
2. `colosseum` is the most exercised theme in the codebase (F3 moments, `postprobe.cjs`, the default arena of many seeds) and has the richest entrance anchors (stands, perches, tunnel, portcullis), so camera and entrance risks show up first.
3. Night + warm/cool split is the signature look of the whole art bible (ref-3, ref-8, ref-9 and the title art).
4. Its landmark is architecture, which the kit and GN generators build reliably; organic landmarks (the tree-city, the colossi) are riskier and come later.

Runner-up: the Frostspire Keep (ref-7), which is daylight and less forgiving of flat lighting; it goes first after the hero.

**Theme order after the hero** (most kit reuse first, new kits last): Frostspire (statues, gothic), Molten Citadel (gothic castle + lava), Moonfall (moon, statues, floating rocks), Shattered Sanctum (floating islands, candles), Celestial Shrine (new Asian pieces), Skullgate Warpit (new wood/bone kit, mud), Rootwood (new theme, foliage, sim change).

**Parallelism:** A3/A4/A5/A6 can run as separate sessions in any order after A1, but Blender jobs must not overlap on the owner's PC, so in practice they run one after another. A19/A20 wait for F3 to land.
